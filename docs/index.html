WHAT IT DOES

Small app. Big sense of time.

Everything you asked for, built into one focused timer app for iPhone and iPad.

iCloud sync

Your timers, custom presets, and alarms travel through your private iCloud key-value store. Start once; see it everywhere.

Live Activities that stay

Running timers live on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. When one ends it stays visible with Restart and X until you dismiss it.

Quick timers

One tap starts 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, or 30 minutes. Add your own presets from seconds to 29 days.

Quick timer widget

Add the small Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, choose a saved quick timer, and tap to start. Pause or stop it right from the widget.

Alarms for the whole day

Set any hour and minute from 12 AM to 12 AM. No seconds, no Live Activities — just the alarm, Stop, and a fixed 9-minute snooze.

Critical alerts

When iOS grants the restricted entitlement, timer and alarm notifications bypass Do Not Disturb, silent mode, and the mute switch at full volume.

A loud custom ding

A 27-second layered bell that repeats like an alarm, just under iOS's 30-second notification sound limit. The same sound is used everywhere.

Roller-picker presets

Days, hours, minutes, and seconds. Slide the wheels up to 29d 23h 59m 59s, tap Done, and it joins your grid.

Multiple timers at once

Run several countdowns together. Each one keeps its own Live Activity, notification, pause state, and restart control.

INSTALL

Build. Download. Sideload.

The IPA on this page is built automatically from the repository by GitHub Actions. It is unsigned, so iOS needs a signing tool such as AltStore, SideStore, or Sideloadly.

01

Download the IPA

Tap Download IPA. You can also install it from the AltStore source on the right.

02

Sign it to your device

Open the IPA with AltStore, SideStore, or Sideloadly and sign it with your Apple ID. A paid developer account is recommended for iCloud entitlements.

03

Trust the developer

On iOS, go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and trust the certificate used to sign the app.

04

Turn on the good stuff

Allow notifications and Live Activities. Sign both devices in to the same Apple ID with iCloud available. For the loudest Do Not Disturb bypass, allow Critical Alerts if your signing tool injected the entitlement; otherwise add Live Timer Connect to Focus → Do Not Disturb → Allowed Apps.

DETAILS

Good to know.

How does sync actually work?

Every change is saved to the app group and published to iCloud's key-value store. Other signed-in devices receive the change, merge it by item timestamp, and reschedule their own Live Activities and notifications. If iCloud is unavailable, the app keeps working locally.

Why does the IPA need signing?

Apple requires every app on a device to be signed. GitHub Actions produces an unsigned IPA because no developer certificate is stored in the repository. AltStore, SideStore, or Sideloadly sign it with your Apple ID when installing.

Do alarms have Live Activities?

No. Alarms use a local notification with the long custom ding. The notification offers Stop and Snooze 9 minutes. Running timers use Live Activities and show pause, stop, restart, and dismiss controls on the Lock Screen.

What happens when a timer ends?

You get a notification named after the timer, such as “5-minute timer has ended,” with the long custom ding. The Live Activity stays visible, says it has ended, and keeps Restart and X buttons until you dismiss it.

How do Critical Alerts work?

The app requests .criticalAlert and uses UNNotificationSound.criticalSoundNamed(_:withAudioVolume: 1.0). When the restricted com.apple.developer.usernotifications.critical-alerts entitlement is present and authorized, notifications bypass Do Not Disturb, silent mode, and the mute switch. Without it, the app falls back to standard time-sensitive notifications and the same sound. Add the app to Focus → Do Not Disturb → Allowed Apps if your signing tool strips the entitlement.

How does the quick timer widget work?

Add the small Home Screen widget or the rectangular Lock Screen widget, then edit it to choose a saved quick timer. When idle it says Tap to Start. While running it shows the remaining time with pause and stop buttons; when finished it offers restart and X.

What are the limits?

Custom quick timers run from 1 second to 29 days, 23 hours, 59 minutes, 59 seconds. Alarms cover all 24 hours at minute precision. The app is built for iOS 17 and later on iPhone and iPad.

Where is the source?

The full SwiftUI project and the GitHub Actions build are at github.com/kingyorkson/livetimerconnect. Every push to main builds a fresh IPA.
